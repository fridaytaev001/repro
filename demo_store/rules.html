DEMO STORE RULES / VERSION 1

Small print.
Clear expectations.

This original demo store uses synthetic products and customers. It contains an intentionally seeded defect for evaluating Repro. No real payment or shipment occurs.

Product and pricing

  • The Everyday Weekender costs $100.00 per unit; quantities 1–3 are supported.
  • US shipping is free. Demo tax is always $0.00.
  • Prices are in USD.

Coupons

  • SAVE10 reduces the merchandise subtotal by 10%, including every supported quantity.
  • Address-only edits, moving between checkout steps, and changing a US address do not invalidate SAVE10.
  • VIP20 requires a member account. The demo cart is a guest cart and is not eligible.
  • Other coupon codes are invalid. An unsuccessful coupon attempt preserves any already applied valid coupon.

Sessions and reset

Cart state is stored in this browser's local storage. A fresh browser context starts with one item and no coupon. “Reset demo session” restores the same state and synthetic shipping fixture. There is no shared server-side cart state.

Reporting

Reports are saved locally by Repro. When Crusoe is configured, the service can investigate with a real browser. Missing configuration is reported as a blocker. Candidate findings still need independent verification; this milestone does not repair code.
